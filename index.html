Why Creatine Increases Water Needs:

Creatine is an osmolyte that draws water intracellularly into skeletal muscle fibers to support ATP energy regeneration and cell volumization. Increasing daily fluid intake by 500–1000ml prevents mild dehydration, muscle cramping, and supports comfortable renal clearance.
